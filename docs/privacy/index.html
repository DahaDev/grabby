Privacy Policy

The short version: nothing.

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Grabby is a local desktop application. It does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal information. There is no account system. There is no analytics. There is no telemetry. There is no advertising.

What Grabby does over the network

The app makes network requests in only two situations, both essential to its function and both initiated by you:

1. Downloading content you ask for. When you paste a URL and click Fetch or Download, Grabby (via the bundled yt-dlp engine) contacts the source platform — YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, etc. — to retrieve the video or audio you requested. Grabby does not log or store the URLs you paste. The source platforms have their own privacy practices, which are outside our control.

2. Checking for updates. Grabby periodically asks GitHub whether a newer version of itself exists. This request includes only generic information GitHub logs for any visitor (IP address, user agent). GitHub's privacy policy applies to this traffic. The check happens on launch and every four hours while the app is open. No personal data leaves your machine.

What Grabby stores locally

The app saves a small settings file on your computer — your preferred download folder, browser cookies preference, and similar settings. This file stays on your machine and is never transmitted anywhere. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or removing the application support folder.

Downloaded files are saved to a folder you choose. We have no record of what you download.

Children's privacy

Grabby does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. We do not collect information from anyone, period.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at info@artofdaha.com.
